Start Broadcast Heart Rate on your Garmin, then connect.

DIRECT FROM YOUR WATCH No account. No uploads.
FĒNIX 6 PRO + IPHONE

Watch → phone. That's it.

  1. Open this page over HTTPS in Bluefy, the Web BLE browser for iPhone. Safari and Chrome on iPhone cannot receive this Bluetooth data.
  2. On your Garmin, open the heart-rate widget, hold MENU, choose Heart Rate Options → Broadcast Heart Rate, then press START.
  3. Tap Connect Garmin here, select your watch, and allow Bluetooth access when prompted.
  4. Turn the phone sideways and keep Bluefy in the foreground. Keep the watch on your wrist.

If the watch is not listed, check that broadcasting is active and the watch's software is up to date. A Bluetooth-only phone cannot receive an ANT+-only broadcast.

Screen wake lock is requested while connected. If it is unavailable, temporarily change iPhone Auto-Lock for your workout, then restore it afterward. Leaving this page can interrupt readings.

Your heart-rate data stays in this page. Nothing is stored or uploaded. This is a workout display, not a medical monitor.
